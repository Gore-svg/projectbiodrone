The problem

Planting trees by hand doesn't scale.

Manual reforestation is slow, expensive, and often dangerous in the remote or fire-damaged terrain that needs it most. We built BioDrone to make seed distribution faster, safer, and cheap enough to actually use at scale.

01

Bushfire recovery

Burnt land is often unstable and hard to walk, making aerial seeding a safer way to kick-start regrowth.

02

Remote terrain

Steep, dense, or inaccessible land that would take a planting crew days can be covered by air in minutes.

03

Cost & labour

One flexible, low-cost platform replaces a lot of manual hours — and the design scales up or down with the job.

The design

Four systems, one airframe.

Built on a drone platform, BioDrone adds a custom 3D-printed hopper and funnel, released through a servo gate that's controlled entirely by an onboard Arduino.

Drone
Arduino housing
Seed storage
Motor housing

The seed hopper sits above a funnel that channels seeds toward a single release point. A micro servo, driven by the Arduino, opens and closes the gate on command — so drop timing and quantity are fully programmable, not just gravity-fed.

Everything is designed modular on purpose: the hopper, funnel, and frame size can all be scaled up or down depending on the payload and the job — reforestation one week, a heavier agricultural payload the next.

Under the hood

Key components

Every part earns its weight — flight, control, and the seed-release system all have to work together without pushing the drone past its payload limit.

CONTROL

Accelerometer & Connected to arduino board

Sends the drone's acceleration data to arduino to chekc whether the drone is stable enough to drop payload.

MOTOR

Flight controller + ESC stack

Keeps the drone stable in the air and translates pilot input into motor speed.

POWER

PowerBank

Powerbank connected to arduino to power all electronics.

LOGIC

Arduino microcontroller

Runs the programmable logic behind the seed-release system.

RELEASE

Servo-gate mechanism

Opens and closes the hole on the disk on command from the Arduino.

PAYLOAD

3D-printed hopper & funnel

Custom-designed to store seeds in flight and funnel them to the release disk.

Comparison

Cheaper than the alternatives.

BioDrone isn't reinventing agricultural or medical automation — it's aiming to make it dramatically more affordable, using an inexpensive FPV platform instead of specialised industrial equipment.

Like other automated seeding and delivery systems, it removes manual labour from the equation. Unlike most, its size, payload, and software are all easy to modify, so the same base platform can be re-purposed for a completely different job.

Safety

Risk assessment

Every build and test session follows a fixed set of precautions. Tap a risk to see how we manage it.

Physical

Cuts or injuries from spinning propellers. We keep clear of props at all times and only arm the drone inside a designated testing area.

Physical

Collision with people, animals, or objects. Flights happen in a large open area, with a safe distance kept from bystanders.

Electrical

Fire, overheating, or battery damage. Batteries are stored in a fireproof bag and inspected before every use.

Physical

Burns from a hot soldering iron. Safety equipment is worn and all soldering is done under supervision.

Electrical

Short circuits or electrical damage. Wiring is checked before power-on, with a smoke stopper used during testing.

Site

Slipping hazards and sharp edges. Seeds are cleaned up after every test, and printed parts are inspected and smoothed beforehand.

How we got here

Our process

From first sketch to a tested prototype — the timeline fills in as you scroll.

Stage 01

Research & concept

Landed on a seed-dropping drone after looking at real reforestation and delivery problems, and scoped the brief around an FPV platform with a custom payload system.

Stage 02

3D design in Fusion 360

Modelled the custom seed hopper and funnel system, iterating on a shape that would feed seeds reliably to a single release point.

Stage 03

Electronics & Arduino

Wired the servo-controlled gate to an Arduino microcontroller and wrote the release logic — the first of several programmable features.

Stage 04

Assembly

Brought the carbon-fibre frame, motors, flight controller, FPV camera, and 3D-printed payload system together into one build.

Stage 05

Drop testing

Ran physical drop tests at different release heights to investigate how height affects seed spread on the ground.

Stage 06

Poster, infographic & this site

Packaged the project for presentation — poster, infographic, and this write-up — ready to share and vote on.

What's next

Future improvements

The modular design was built to be extended — here's what we'd add next.

LCD status display

An onboard screen to show battery level, seed count, and release status without needing a separate device.

GPS-guided flight paths

Pre-programmed routes so seed release can follow a planned grid instead of relying purely on manual piloting.

Larger, swappable payload

A bigger hopper variant for heavier jobs — agricultural seed or small medical supply runs — without redesigning the frame.

Weatherproofing

Sealing the electronics and hopper so test flights aren't limited to dry, calm conditions.
